Background

From psychology to product.

I completed a BS in Psychology in 2025. My undergraduate thesis examined the relationship between romantic-relationship breakup, rumination and life satisfaction among young adults — a quantitative study using correlation analysis, the Kruskal–Wallis H test, normality checks and reliability analysis in SPSS. It was commended at the Eleventh Applied Business Research Conference.

Alongside that I completed two clinical placements, where most of what I learned was about interviewing: how the wording of a question changes the answer, and how much a person tells you when you stop filling the silence.

Psychology gave me the methods and nothing to change with them. Product work is where a finding about attention, effort or trust becomes something a person actually uses — and where the claim gets tested rather than filed away. The work on this site is my own bridge across: two studies with instruments I wrote, two prototypes built from what they found, and an evaluation designed to tell me if I was wrong.
